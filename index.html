Changelog

What's new

v11.0
  • New: version 11. This version brings together the pre-match lobby (10 seconds of waiting where everyone is immune and the players start spread out, with an animated battle bus countdown) and the new battle bus with a hot air balloon on top, which replaces the plane.
v10.3
  • Change: the plane is gone. You now jump from a blue battle bus with a big colorful hot air balloon on top, with its own shadow on the ground.
v10.2
  • Change: in the pre-match lobby the players now start much more spread out, so you are not in the middle of a crowd.
  • Change: the lobby now only shows the battle bus message, with an animated bus driving along a track and a big countdown that pops every second. The extra text about immunity is gone.
v10.1
  • New: pre-match lobby, like Fortnite. At the start of every match there are 10 seconds of waiting for the battle bus. You can move and fight (pickaxe and soldier active), but everyone is immune and nobody takes damage. Chests and loot are locked until the bus arrives. A blue ring shows the immunity and the top message shows the countdown.
v10.0
  • Change: skins are gone. The Outfits tab is removed from the LOCKER and the ITEM SHOP, and profiles no longer show an outfit. Your look is now the one of your chosen soldier.
  • Change: "Brawlers" are now called "Soldiers" everywhere.
  • New: 15 soldiers (Rex, Spark, Doc, Brute, Bandit, Zen, Bastion, Trapper, Hawk, Fury, Shade, Boom, Frost, Breeze, Mirage), each with its own face, size, body shape, skin and colors, rarity, passive and active. The look shows in the menu, in the cards and in the match.
  • New: SOLDIERS button in the menu bar, next to LOCKER. Tap a soldier to see its passive and active and to SELECT it. Only Rex is unlocked at the start.
  • New: the passives and actives of the 15 soldiers work. The passive is always on. The active has a recharge (20 to 35 seconds, the stronger ones take longer) and does not use ammo. Press E on PC, or tap the round button on mobile, to use it. You can move and resize the button in HUD LAYOUT, and change the PC key in SETTINGS.
  • Change: the Hair dryer is gone from chests and from the map. It now only exists as the active of Breeze. The accessory slot (key 5) and the ACC button are removed.
  • Change: Solo, Duos and Squads now have 100 players on the big map, like Ranked. Duos are 50 teams of 2 and Squads are 25 teams of 4. Quick Match (20 players) is gone.
  • Change: Solo, Duos and Squads use your soldier and its abilities, and give trophies. Ranked keeps the rank points (Wood to Champion), with the default soldier look, no passive, no active and no trophies. Guests can play, but trophies are not saved.
  • New: trophies. Every soldier has its own, and your total is the sum of all of them. You win or lose trophies in Solo, Duos and Squads, by your place and by your eliminations (the first 10 of each match count). You never go below 0. Duos give half of the Solo trophies and Squads give a quarter.
  • New: prestige. A soldier gets one prestige for every 1000 of its trophies, with no limit (P4 = 4000, P12 = 12000...). A prestige never goes back: from P1 on, a soldier can no longer fall below 1000 x its prestige in trophies. The higher the prestige, the fewer trophies you earn. Each new prestige gives 100 coins once (500 on P5, P10, P15...).
  • New: the prestige badge, a diamond with the number inside. P1 to P4 are plain bronze, silver, gold and ice blue, P5 to P9 have small wings, and P10 and above have a crown and a pulsing glow. It shows on the soldier cards, next to the names in the match and on the leaderboard.
  • New: TROPHY ROAD in the menu bar. It uses the total trophies of all your soldiers. 10 milestones (10, 60, 500, 2000, 3000, 5000, 8000, 10000, 12000 and 15000) unlock Spark, Doc, Brute, Bandit, Zen, Bastion, Trapper, Hawk, Fury and Shade when you CLAIM them. An unlocked soldier stays unlocked.
  • Change: the SOLDIERS screen shows the trophies and the prestige of every soldier, your total at the top, the current elimination rule of each soldier and how many trophies are left for its next prestige.
  • New: the PROFILE shows your total trophies and one card per soldier: the soldier name, a prestige card with the number on top, its face and its trophies.
  • Change: the leaderboard now has a TROPHIES tab, which is the first one and sorts by the total trophies of all your soldiers. The RANKED tab is still there and sorts by rank points.
  • New: BOXES. There are two kinds. The small box costs 100 XP (the same XP as the Battle Pass: 1 per elimination, 15 per win; you get one box for every 100 XP, and your Battle Pass levels do not go down). The big box needs 10 victories: every victory fills 1/10 of the box, with an animation on the result screen. Boxes need an account.
  • New: the boxes are the only way to get Boom and Frost (Epic), Breeze (Mythic) and Mirage (Legendary). Chances for each box: Epic 1%, Mythic 0.1%, Legendary 0.01%. Every box opened without a soldier raises each chance by 1% of its current value, and it stacks. When a soldier drops, the chances of that box go back to the start.
  • New: CHANCES BY RARITY button in BOXES. It shows your current chance of winning each soldier (Epic, Mythic, Legendary) in the small and big box.
  • New: no soldier means coins: 10 from a small box, 50 from a big box. If you already own the soldier, you get coins instead (50 Epic, 150 Mythic, 500 Legendary) and the chances restart too.
  • New: opening screen. The box shakes, opens, glows with the colour of the rarity and shows the prize.
  • New: BOXES in the menu bar, and the main menu shows the small box AND the big box, each with its own icon, amount and progress bar.
  • New: server saving. Trophies of every soldier, unlocked soldiers, prestige, Trophy Road rewards already claimed, small and big boxes, the big box progress and the box chance counters are saved on the server, like coins and the Battle Pass.
  • Change: the server calculates trophies, prestige coins, Trophy Road rewards and box prizes by itself. Editing your device does not change them.
  • Change: the online leaderboard sorts by your total trophies, and the server keeps that number up to date.
  • New: the first time you connect, your existing progress is sent to the server once, so nothing you already earned is lost.
  • Change: if the server is not available, the game keeps saving on your device. Matches played offline are sent to the server the next time it connects. Opening boxes needs the server once your account has used it.
  • Change: the Battle Pass level 150 reward is now 100 coins instead of the Champion skin.
  • Fix: coins from boxes, prestiges and chests found on the map are no longer lost when the game syncs with the server.
v8.1
  • New: every chest you open has a 1% chance to hold 10 coins. They burst out of the chest with a sound and go straight to your balance, so you can spend them in the ITEM SHOP.
  • New: the game now asks you to rotate your phone to landscape as soon as it opens.
v8.0
  • Change: the storm now hurts more the farther you are from the safe zone.
  • New: ammo. Bullets are picked up automatically when you walk over them, and reloading uses them. Found in chests, buildings and around the map. You start with 60.
  • New: on mobile, press and hold the aim stick without sliding to autofire at the nearest enemy, aiming where it was 200 ms ago. Slide it to aim by hand as before.
  • Change: Med kits and Shields now heal automatically no matter how much health you have (Auto heal ON).
  • Change: the Shotgun and the Sniper fire 500 ms after you start aiming, also with autofire.
  • New: weapons you are not holding reload on their own.
  • Change: the Shotgun reloads one shell at a time, and you can fire while it reloads.
v7.0
  • New: accessories. A new slot (key 5) holds an accessory, which is not a weapon or a heal.
  • New: the Hair dryer, the first accessory. It blows wind and pushes you the opposite way you aim, so you can use it to move. It also pushes bots in front of you. Found in chests and around the map.
  • New: round buttons on mobile to swap Pickaxe / weapon and weapon / accessory. They can be moved and resized in HUD LAYOUT.
  • New: on mobile, the first shot after you start aiming comes out 100 ms later.
  • Fix: you can now swap a weapon for the same weapon with a different rarity.
  • Fix: HUD LAYOUT now brings buttons that were off the screen back into view.
v6.0
  • New: houses have roofs, so you cannot see inside until you walk in. The roof fades away when you enter, and bots and loot inside stay hidden from outside.
  • New: rooms inside houses, with furniture, and bigger hotels with a lobby desk, several rooms and extra chests.
  • New: cities and towns have streets and a hotel, and roads link the places on the map. Roads show on the minimap.
  • Fix: the RELOAD button can now be moved and resized in HUD LAYOUT, like the other buttons.
  • Ranked: the map is now twice as big, with 12 new places, including 3 more cities, a resort and more towns.
v5.8
  • Fix: the game no longer pauses when you switch to another tab or window on PC. The match keeps running in the background, with the sound muted.
v5.7
  • New: a white aim line shows how far your weapon reaches. The shotgun shows its spread as an angle.
v5.6
  • Security: coins, Battle Pass XP, daily missions, rewards, shop purchases and equipped items are now validated and saved on the server. Editing your device does not change them.
v5.5
  • Security: coins, Battle Pass progress, items and stats are now signed. If saved data or an exported account file is edited, it is detected and reset.
v5.4
  • Change: in Duos and Squads you can no longer be revived once the final zone has closed. A revive timer that already started keeps counting.
v5.3
  • Balance: SMG damage x1.3, Rifle damage x1.3, Shotgun damage x2.0.
v5.2
  • New: weapon rarities (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary). Higher rarity means more damage and faster reload. Chests give better odds.
  • Change: teammate names are now red in Duos and Squads.
  • Change: the Battle Pass has a BACK button at the top and live timers.
v5.1
  • New: free BATTLE PASS with 150 levels (100 XP each) and a season timer that ends on 1 Jan 2027, 00:00 Germany.
  • New: 3 daily missions (1 x 150 XP, 2 x 50 XP) that reset at 0:00 Germany time.
  • New: XP from 1 per kill and 15 per win. Rewards include sprays, a few coins, a pickaxe at level 50 and a skin at level 150.
  • Change: coins are now earned only through the Battle Pass.
v5.0
  • New: Fortnite-style main menu with PLAY, LOCKER, ITEM SHOP, LEADERBOARD and SETTINGS. Pick your mode next to PLAY and see your skin in the lobby.
  • New: the LOCKER only equips what you own and the ITEM SHOP is where you spend coins.
  • New: profiles (yours and other players' on the leaderboard) show the equipped outfit, pickaxe, glider, spray, weapon color and elimination effect.
  • Changed: you choose PC or MOBILE once when the game opens (change it in SETTINGS), not before each match.
v4.3
  • New: game coins. You earn them after every match (kills, placement and time survived, more in Ranked).
  • New: LOCKER. Buy and equip outfits, pickaxes, gliders, sprays, weapon colors and elimination effects.
  • New: sprays in match (T on PC, SPRAY button on mobile, rebindable in SETTINGS). Everyone starts with the RRoyal spray.
  • New: every player now shows their name above their character, and bots get random names each match.
v4.2
  • New: KEYBINDS is now SETTINGS. You can rebind the controller buttons there (click an action, then press the new button on your pad), and turn Auto heal ON or OFF.
v4.1
  • New: controller support on PC and phone (Xbox, PlayStation and most Bluetooth or USB pads). Left stick moves, right stick aims and shoots, RT shoots, A jumps and picks up, B builds a wall, X reloads, Y swaps Pickaxe and weapon, LB uses a Med kit, RB uses a Shield, Back opens the map. Press any button once so the browser detects the pad.
v4.0
  • New: rank animation at the end of a Ranked match. Your soldier faces the screen, the RP counts up or down next to your badge, and when your rank changes the badge flashes and swaps.
v3.9
  • New: auto heal. Your Med kit is used on its own when your health drops below 50, and a Shield when your shield is at 50 or less. It stops when you shoot or swing, and starts again when you stop.
v3.8
  • New (PC): scroll the mouse wheel to switch between the Pickaxe and your weapon. You can also click the slots of the item bar with the mouse.
v3.7
  • New: every rank has its own badge (Wood, Stone, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Master, Champion). It shows in your Profile, in other players' profiles and next to every name on the leaderboard. The 3 dots under the badge show your division.
v3.6
  • New: Med kits and Shields are picked up automatically when you walk over them (up to 3 of each).
  • New: damage numbers pop up when you hit an enemy. A red bigger number means a kill.
  • Fix: the Shield and Med kit counts in the item bar were cut off. They now show clearly (x0 to x3) with the name below.
v3.5
  • New: weapon panel above the item bar with the weapon name, bullets left and a RELOADING status with a progress bar.
  • New: the Shield and HP bars now show their exact numbers.
v3.4
  • New: if you sign in with Google, you can change your name any time in your Profile. Names already taken are blocked.
v3.3
  • New: the online leaderboard now works on the website, and it saves your rank points for the Ranked tab.
v3.2
  • New: sign in with Google from your Profile, no password needed.
v3.1
  • Fix: in Ranked, some players stayed counted as alive at the end even though they were not in the match. The plane now only leaves once everyone has jumped.
  • New: in your Profile you can see your password with a SHOW button, in case you forget it. If it is empty, type your password once to save it.
v3.0
  • New: PLAY now asks for Ranked or Quick Match.
  • Ranked Battle Royale: 100 players on a map about 5x bigger, with 8 ranks (Wood, Stone, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Master, Champion), 3 divisions each. The higher you are, the less you gain and the more you lose.
  • Leaderboard has a Ranked tab. Tap any player to open their full profile and stats.
  • Weapons now have magazines and reload (R key, or the Reload button on mobile). They also reload on their own when empty.
  • Carry up to 3 Shields and 3 Med kits. Shield takes 2 s. Med kit takes 10 s and heals 10% of your health per second (4 = Shield, 3 = Kit).
  • Map key (M, or tap the minimap) shows where you are and the whole island.
  • Fixed, named towns: cities, farms, barns and villages that are always in the same place.
  • Trees are now solid. They block movement and bullets, so use them as cover.
  • Fix: logging in with the right password now works, including on another device.
v2.0
  • Weapons now come in chests. Walk up to a chest to open it and grab what drops. Chests glow gold on the map and the minimap until opened.
  • Bigger map (about 30% larger) with 9 villages of different sizes, plus lone houses.
  • Trees added for style, just decoration, so they never block you.
  • The shotgun has a new pump-action design, with a barrel, magazine tube and wooden pump.
  • Fix: bots no longer get stuck on rocks, and rocks are spaced so no pockets can trap anyone.
v1.4
  • New: HUD LAYOUT in the main menu. Drag the MOVE and AIM / FIRE sticks, the jump, pick-up and wall buttons, the item bar, the minimap and the info panel anywhere, and resize each one. Your layout is saved on your device.
v1.3
  • New: a SHOW / HIDE button next to the password field when you create an account or log in.
v1.2
  • Fix: bots no longer shoot at you before they are on your screen.
v1.1
  • New main skin: an original soldier with blond hair, scarf, tactical vest, red armband and backpack.
  • Bots got their own look, with different hair and vest colors.
  • Weapons redrawn with a 3D look: shading, highlights, scopes, magazines and wooden stocks.
  • Weapons on the ground are bigger and glow in their tier color.
  • Version label and this What's new screen.
v1.0
  • Launch: 2D battle royale for 20 players, with Solo, Duos and Squads.
  • Bots no longer get stuck inside houses.
  • Teammate revive in Duos (15 s) and Squads (30 s) while an ally is alive.
  • Accounts and profile with games, wins and kills, plus save, download and import.
  • Online leaderboard for kills and wins.
  • Rebindable keys on PC and a smaller pick-up button.
  • Whole game in English.
